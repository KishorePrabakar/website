Study Protocol & Workflow
Fast reference for problem solving + spaced repetition
1
Priming 5–10m
Read constraints, check bounds, and trace 1–2 test cases by hand.
2
Attempt Window 15–30m
Easy: 15m | Medium: 25m | Hard: 35m. If stuck when the timer ends, stop and review solution.
3
Review & Refactor 10–15m
Compare solution time/space complexity and rewrite cleanly.
4
Note Taking 3–5m
Log 3 things: Core Insight, Pitfalls/Traps, and Pattern Keyword.
5
Spaced Repetition Schedule
Log R1–R4 reviews and track progress after each stage.
Spaced Repetition Cadence
R1 (1 Day): Re-code from scratch <10 mins
R2 (3 Days): Write pseudocode or explain logic out loud
R3 (7 Days): Full re-code without looking at hints
R4 (30 Days): Final mastery check
Reset Rule: If you fail any review stage (stuck >15m or forgot pattern), reset to Day 0.
